Sources & method

What is measured

Distance is the straight-line (haversine, great-circle) distance in km from the castle reference point to the station's platform centroid. It is not walking or rail distance. A WGS84 ellipsoid (geodesic) value is in the data for cross-checking; the two agree within 0.6% on every row.

Main station: the town's principal JR or namesake station. Central station: the heavy-rail, subway or monorail station nearest the castle inside the town (trams excluded), with manual picks for 本八戸, 東岡崎, 行田市, 上野市, 近鉄郡山, 水口城南, 松山市, 西鉄福岡（天神）, 膳所 and 東萩. In many towns the two are the same station.

Castle point: Wikidata P625, Japanese Wikipedia GeoData, or OpenStreetMap (source on every row). Mountain castles (yamajiro) are measured from the honmaru / summit.

Population, area, density: 2020 Population Census (令和2年国勢調査, e-Stat table 1-1) for the current municipality. Former-town figures are shown in the detail panel where the census has them (post-2000 mergers only).

Merged towns: MLIT N03 boundaries (2000, 1950; 1920 for 長府 and 飫肥) identify castle towns later absorbed into another city. These former-town rows are measured to a station inside the old town; if it has none, the row is no rail and is listed but not plotted.

Compare to Matsue: ratio of each town's population or density to Matsue's (203,616 people; 355.4 /km², 2020). The band mode uses the dataset rule: both = 0.5–1.5× Matsue on population and density.

Summary panel numbers are recomputed in your browser for the current filter and station. Spearman ρ there has no confidence interval; the bootstrapped figures below come from stats_v2.json.

    Known limits and judgement calls

    • 千代田区 (Edo Castle, ward-level statistics) and 京都市 (Nijō) are kept as special cases; 首里城 (a gusuku, not a jōkamachi) is kept and measured to Yui Rail 首里駅. They are flagged in each row.
    • Jinya (陣屋) cover about 19 of 100+ jinya han seats; 7 more have no coordinate yet and are not in the table.
    • Which station counts as "main" or "central" is a rule, not a fact; ridership was not checked.
    • Population for towns absorbed before 2000 is unknown at town level (not zero); charts use current-municipality figures.
    • Castle status defaults to ruins where the Wikipedia infobox has no tower field (flagged). About 93 castle points have only one coordinate source.
    • Festivals are a draft from Wikipedia city articles, unverified and not ranked; not researched for v2 additions.
